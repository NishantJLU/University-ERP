# Integrated LMS: Canvas Simplicity & D2L Brightspace Analytics Depth

## Design Philosophy

The Learning Management System (LMS) is not a disconnected external application. It is natively embedded into the university's ERP:
- **Academic Subject Linkage**: An academic subject created by the administrator automatically becomes an LMS Course.
- **Instructor Auto-Provisioning**: When faculty is assigned to a course section in the ERP, they are automatically granted instructor privileges in the corresponding LMS course.
- **Student Enrollment Sync**: All students enrolled in an academic semester section are automatically registered in the LMS course.

## Canvas-Inspired User Experience
- Clean, focused navigation tabs: Course Modules, Assignments, Quizzes, and Gradebook.
- Side To-Do list reminding students of upcoming deadlines and exams.
- Simple, friction-free assignment submission and feedback review.

## D2L Brightspace Depth & Academic Analytics
- **Learning Objectives & Competencies**: Tracks continuous mastery across curricular units (e.g. Relational Normalization, Index Structures).
- **Progress Tracking**: Tracks percentage of lessons reviewed and resources downloaded.
- **Continuous Evaluation Gradebook**: Weighted breakdown across assignments (20%), quizzes (10%), mid-terms (30%), and end-terms (40%).

## Native ERP Telemetry in the Course Hub
- Real-time subject-specific attendance percentage displayed directly in the course header.
- Next lecture time and room location pulled dynamically from the master published timetable.
